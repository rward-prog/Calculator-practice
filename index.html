STO▸, MODE

Like the real calculator: typing on top of a character replaces it (press 2nd DEL to insert instead), DEL at the very end of a line does nothing, and 2nd ▲ / 2nd ▼ change the screen contrast. Press MODE to switch between Radian and Degree, or Normal, Sci and Eng.

For ALPHA letters (like the A in 5 STO▸ A), use the on-screen ALPHA key.
